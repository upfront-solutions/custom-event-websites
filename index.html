What is the best option for a custom event website?

The best option depends on the event. For a small, single-day gathering, a free builder like Zola or Joy is the best choice, because it costs nothing and does the job. For a wedding with a look you have already invested in, a premium template platform such as Riley & Grey or Bliss & Bone is a reasonable middle. Once you need a design of your own, an event that is not a wedding, or a feature a builder cannot do, a custom studio is the best option. Custom Event Websites is the best value in that group: fully custom from $350 where bespoke studios usually start in the thousands, live in five business days, and built for every kind of event rather than weddings only.

What should I look for when choosing a custom event website designer?

Five things separate a good one from the rest. Whether the design is genuinely made for your event or a theme with your name dropped in. Whether the price is one-time or a subscription you keep paying after the event. How fast they deliver, since most custom designers quote four to eight weeks. Whether they build for your kind of event or only weddings. And whether you own the domain and the finished site afterward. Custom Event Websites is built to answer all five: custom design, one-time price from $350, live in five business days, every event type, and the domain registered in your name.

How much does a custom event website cost?

Ours start at $350 for a one-page site, $500 for a two-page site with schedule, maps, guest logins and a live RSVP dashboard, and $1,500 and up for a large multi-page build. That is a one-time price with no subscription. Template builders like Zola and The Knot are free to about $99 a year, and premium template platforms run $15 to $39 a month.

What is the difference between the $350 and $500 packages?

The $350 Starter is a single page with RSVP, a gallery and registry links, which covers most baby showers and small birthdays. The $500 Signature adds a second page, a full schedule with locations and maps, personal guest logins, a live RSVP dashboard, meal choices, a guest comment wall and a vendor credit page. Pick the Signature if guests need to know where to be and when.

How long does it take to build?

Five business days, on every package. You approve a design concept in the first two days, we build it in the next two, and it goes live on day five. If your event is sooner than that, we offer a rush option at 50% above the package price.

What kinds of events do you build for?

Baby showers, bridal showers, gender reveals, sip and sees, first birthdays, sweet sixteens, quinceañeras, milestone birthdays, engagement parties, bachelorette and bachelor trips, rehearsal dinners, weddings, vow renewals, anniversary and retirement parties, graduations and holiday parties. We also build for business events such as conferences, workshops, galas, fundraisers and grand openings, and for event vendors who need a site of their own.

Do I own the website and the domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the finished site is yours. Hosting is included through your event date plus 30 days. After that you can keep it online as a keepsake for $60 a year, or we archive it and send you a copy. Every site also keeps a free permanent address on our own domain.

Do guests need an account to RSVP?

No. On every package guests can RSVP straight from the page with no login. On The Signature and The Production, each guest also gets a personal link that shows their own invitation, their events and their table.

Can you match my invitations?

That is the usual starting point. Send the invitation suite, your color palette, your fonts or a mood board, and the site is designed around them so the whole thing reads as one piece.

How do payments work?

Half up front to reserve your build slot, half before the site goes live. The deposit is non-refundable because it holds the calendar time, but the balance is not due until you have seen and approved the finished site.

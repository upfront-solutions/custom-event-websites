how the morning goes

The plan

11:00 Splash time

Warm shallow pool, floats and tails in every size, and two lifeguards in the water the whole time.

11:45 Tide pool touch tank

Sea stars, urchins and one very patient hermit crab named Gerald. Hands in, gently.

12:30 Smash cake and lunch

Sandwiches and fruit for the grown ups, and one small cake that Sophie is going to destroy on camera.

1:00 Bubbles and pearls

A bubble machine, soft music, and paper pearls scattered for the big siblings to find.

2:00 Nap time

Shell bags by the door, one for every little one.

What to wear and bring

Wear

  • Blue, green, purple or anything that sparkles
  • A swimsuit underneath, already on
  • Tails are welcome, and we have spares
  • Shoes that do not mind getting wet

Bring

  • A towel with a name on it
  • Swim nappies if your little one is still in them
  • A dry change of clothes for the ride home
  • Nothing else, we have the rest covered

our story

Elena was hosting an art show. Andrei was one of the vendors. He fell for her that night and asked everyone there how to get a hold of her.

He finally got her number and asked her on a date. The rest is history.

Six years later their friends are one blended group, and they are so excited to share the day with every one of you.

the good bits

Tap through the day
From 3:00

A painter at the gate

Marguerite sets up an easel by the white gate and paints the crowd as it arrives. The canvas comes home with us.

From 3:00

Something cold

Lemonade, iced tea and a very good peach shrub, handed to you before you reach your seat.

All day

The Polaroid book

Take a camera, take a picture, stick it in the book and write something we can read back in thirty years.

4:00

The oyster cart

Shucked to order in the pear orchard, with mignonette and a lot of lemon.

4:00

Lawn games

Bocce, croquet and a ring toss that has caused arguments at two previous family weddings.

4:30

A string trio

Three players from Kingston under the big pear tree. Requests welcome, within reason.

5:30

Dinner, family style

Long tables, big platters, pass everything to your left. No assigned seats and no head table.

8:00

Six-piece band

Horns and all. They take requests, and you can leave yours on the reply form below.

9:00

The cake

Four tiers, olive oil and lemon, built by the baker who made Elena’s first birthday cake.

11:00

Late night burgers

A burger van pulls into the field at eleven and stays until the last person gives up. Fries included, obviously.

11:30

Pierogi and pickles

Elena’s grandmother’s recipe, served on the back porch by people who have made it a hundred times.

1:00

Sparklers at the gate

Everybody out, everybody lit, one photograph, then the shuttle.

Questions

What should I wear?

Black tie optional. The lawn is soft, so flats or block heels.

Can I bring someone?

If your invitation says "and guest", yes.

Are children invited?

To the ceremony and cocktails. A sitter is at the farmhouse from 5:30.

Is there parking?

Free in the lower hayfield, attended until one. Leave the car overnight.

Any gifts?

Your presence is the present. If you insist, there is a fund for a week in Puglia.

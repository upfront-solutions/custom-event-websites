How it works

  1. You offer a custom website to your client.
  2. You charge your client the price you want to charge.
  3. You pay us the discounted rate we agreed on with you.
  4. You keep the difference.
  5. We make the website.

That is it. You do not need to make the website or learn how to make one. We do all of that work.

You can stay in the middle, or hand your client to us

Some planners want to handle it themselves. You send us the details, we build the website, and it comes back to you to give to your client.

Some planners would rather not deal with it. You introduce us to your client, we work with them directly, and you still get paid.

Either one works. Tell us which you prefer and we will set it up that way.

The website is hosted with us either way. That part does not change.

What the websites include

Each website is made for that one event. It is not a template.

It can include an RSVP page, a schedule, maps and locations, photos, registry links, meal choices, and other pages your client needs.

We build the website in 5 business days.

Questions

How much is the discounted rate?

We work that out with you when you contact us. It depends on your business. We will agree on it before you offer a website to any client, so you will know what you will pay and what you can make.

Do I set the price for my client?

Yes, if you stay in the middle. You choose what to charge your client, you pay us the discounted rate, and you keep the rest. If you would rather we work with your client directly, we agree on your payment before we start.

Who makes the website?

We do. You offer it to your client and we design it, build it, and finish it.

What events can this be for?
